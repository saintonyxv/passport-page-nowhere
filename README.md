The open passport spread of the **Republic of Velmara** — a landlocked mountain republic of high passes and one river, the Sora, under the motto *"Sloboda nad oblaki"* ("Freedom above the clouds"). Its arms are three silver peaks and a gold star on azure; its capital is Veligrad; its language, Velmarski, shares every label on the data page. The passport belongs to Andrej V. Sorin of Veligrad, issued 03 JUN 2021, and its visas page tells his travels in order: Dravania in September 2022, Ostrelia in May 2024, Dravania again in January 2025 — every stamp inside the passport's validity, and the green High-Pass Endorsement of the Warden of Passes (guide required above 3,000 m, fee 40 kruna) stamped the day it was issued, because in Velmara even the paperwork has altitude. `main.py` draws the whole spread procedurally with PIL — guilloche security paper, coat of arms, portrait and ghost image, bilingual data fields, an ICAO-checksummed machine-readable zone, and ink-worn border stamps — and writes it to `/output/entry.png`.
